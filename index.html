BLAIR

I've been messing around with HTML and CSS for over ten years now as well as very mild video game modding (and modding mods!) and involvement with FOSS communities, Linux more recently. I write, on and off, and lately have been taking up Blender; you'll probably notice I tend to pick skills that drive people to the brink of insanity There's nothing of major interest to show off at the moment, but in the future I'd like to use all of these towards reviving in-browser interactive fiction with point and click elements. Old point and click games - especially the weirder ones like The Dark Eye and Planet Laika - have always had a pretty major foothold in my brain. With how accessible JS is these days, there's no reason why these can't see some kind of renaissance. Not only that, but despite the popularity of virtual pet games back in the day and people frequently saying they wish Neopets would come back, there isn't really much out there at the moment. Unless you want to go down the mobile free to play route. And why not use the basis of point and click interactive fiction and virtual pets for more practical purposes? There's absolutely grounds for creating museum-like resources online, especially in the age of AI misinformation and perpetual lack of public interest in scientific fields.

There has only been six known total Spinosaurus fossils that provide us with a picture of what it may have looked like. Most fossils have been largely incomplete and only two species have been identified - the original holotype was destroyed in WW2. The newest species discovered this year has a bladed horn on the top of its head, which builds on the theory that the Spinosaurus was closer to a wading bird in niche and behavior. The crest likely functioned as a massive billboard for other species and its own - look at me! I went to bed thinking about wading birds that night and Spinosauri (?) resembling the Roseate Spoonbill and Scarlet Ibis, then dreamt Ian Malcolm leaned over to me and told me raptors evolved into spiders and I got so angry at the idea that I woke up.
